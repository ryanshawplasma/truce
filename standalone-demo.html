Questions

Before you say sorry

How does delivery work?

It's a link — that's the whole magic. When your card is ready we hand you a private URL. Text it, email it, AirDrop it, print the QR onto a napkin. They tap it and the experience opens right in their browser. Nothing to download, nothing to sign up for.

Can I edit my card after paying?

Yes. Every word stays yours. Reopen the maker, fix the typo you spotted three seconds after sending, and the same link updates. We'd suggest doing it before they open it, but we're not going to judge.

Is it private?

Very. Your card lives at an unguessable link, it's never indexed by search engines, and there's no public gallery of other people's apologies. Only someone with the link can open it — so be a little careful about which group chat you paste it into.

What's your refund policy?

We can't guarantee forgiveness — but our track record is pretty good. If the card itself doesn't work the way it should, email us within 14 days and we'll refund it, no interrogation. If the apology doesn't land, that part is genuinely on you (and time, and probably a real conversation).

Does it work on any phone?

If it has a browser, yes. iPhone, Android, tablets, laptops, the ancient one in the kitchen drawer. Everything is designed mobile-first, loads in a second, and works fine on a shaky signal.
